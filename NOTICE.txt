Clipjump-Ultra ===========

Fork of Clipjump-plus
Copyright (C) 2026 Erhan Tuna
Licensed under Apache License v2.0
https://github.com/magomic/Clipjump-Ultra

Modifications in this fork include: Windows 11 (Fluent) design with
light/dark theme, formatted rich text and syntax highlighted preview,
search in the preview, password clip protection (DPAPI encryption,
masking, automatic deletion), text correction and translation with a
local LLM, read aloud with ElevenLabs, image to Base64 paste, the Clip
Actions window and an extended Settings window.

The modified files carry a notice stating that they were changed.
